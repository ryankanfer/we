## Design Context

### Users
WE is for committed couples with full, overlapping lives, beginning with Ryan and Dylan and later expanding to busy dual-career couples in dense urban environments. They use it to notice what needs attention, coordinate the life they carry, preserve private reflection, and open sensitive topics only through mutual consent. The product should reduce management work and repeated conversations, not create another relationship task system.

### Brand Personality
Calm, intimate, observant, restrained, and quietly premium. WE should sound warm without becoming therapeutic, sentimental, overly cheerful, diagnostic, or managerial. It describes evidence and patterns gently, offers one useful next move, and makes “not now” emotionally safe.

### Aesthetic Direction
A flagship native iPhone experience with a warm editorial character: cream and cinematic warm-dark canvases, meaningful serif typography, restrained sans-serif metadata, burgundy and sage relationship identities, generous space, and minimal decoration. The relationship is one continuous home. Motion should feel like resuming a conversation: soft, purposeful, and state-aware. Avoid glossy productivity dashboards, generic glassmorphism, scores, streaks, gamification, feature density, loud gradients, and decorative animation.

### Design Principles
1. Make the relationship feel continuous. Surfaces should reveal what matters now instead of making two people navigate organizational categories.
2. Make privacy visible through interaction. Invitations, waiting, private answers, mutual reveal, decline, and withdrawal must each feel distinct and safe.
3. Let motion explain state. Use restrained transitions to show opening, holding, joining, resolving, and returning; honor Reduce Motion everywhere.
4. Translate chaos into calm. Prefer one legible next action, progressive disclosure, and quiet hierarchy over dashboards or dense controls.
5. Become quieter with trust. WE should feel less demanding as it learns, never use urgency, scoring, streaks, or guilt to manufacture engagement.
