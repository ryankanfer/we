## Design Context

### Users
Couples using WE privately on iPhone to capture thoughts, save ideas, make shared plans, and understand their relationship. Today shows what matters now, Life holds what they are building, and Us reflects their shared world. Privacy belongs to each object; Yours remains an optional reflection space.

### Brand Personality
Warm, intimate, observant. Premium editorial restraint, calm and slightly playful. The intended recognition is “That is so us,” without analytics, judgment, invented emotional claims, or gamification.

### Aesthetic Direction
Creator-provided references: warm cream canvas, generous negative space, expressive serif typography, quiet burgundy/sage accents, connected “So Us” and “New to Us” territories, and a restrained glass capture panel. Keep native navigation. Avoid dashboards, dense clouds, rainbow colors, arbitrary rotation, and decorative visualizations.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve clarity and deliberate sharing; visible privacy travels with the object.
2. Typography and spacing carry hierarchy; use existing Field tokens.
3. Explanations use actual permitted evidence.
4. Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and Reduce Transparency.
5. Polish toward the established premium quality bar without expanding release scope.
