# First install: the two of you, both phones

WE 1.0 (2). Do this once, together, before either of you writes anything real.

Use invented content for everything below. Not because the app is fragile, but
because you are about to check whether private stays private, and the honest
way to find out is with something you would not mind being wrong about.

Record each result **per phone**. "It worked" from one of you is half an
answer — most of what is worth catching here is a difference between the two.

---

## Before you start

- Both phones on **iOS 26.2 or later**. The app will not install below that.
- Both of you accept the TestFlight invitation and install **the same build**.
  Check Settings → About: it must say **1.0 (2)** on both. A mismatch invalidates
  everything below.
- Decide who is Partner A (creates the space) and who is Partner B (joins).

---

## 1. Pairing

This is first because nothing else can be trusted until it is right.

- [ ] **A**: sign up, and finish the walkthrough or skip it. Either is fine —
      do one of each between you so both paths get used.
- [ ] **A**: no example content from the walkthrough appears in the real
      account afterwards. If any does, stop and report it.
- [ ] **A**: create the space, get the invitation.
- [ ] **B**: sign up on the other phone and redeem it.
- [ ] Both: arrival completes without either of you explaining anything to the
      other. If one of you had to be told what to tap, that is a finding — note
      the screen.
- [ ] Both: you can each find **Yours**, **Account**, **Search** and the
      **Calendar** without help.

> Push notifications are **not** in this build. B's arrival will not appear on
> A's lock screen; A sees it the next time they open the app. That is expected,
> not a bug.

## 2. Private versus shared — the one that matters most

- [ ] **A**: write something in **Yours**. Invented.
- [ ] **B**: look everywhere. Yours, Life, Us, Search, the Calendar, the widget.
      **B must not be able to find it, by any route.** If B can see it or any
      trace of it — a count, a date, a hint that something exists — stop
      everything and report it immediately.
- [ ] **A**: before saving anything, can you tell from the screen whether it is
      private or shared? Say honestly whether you were *sure* or *guessed*.
      Guessing is a finding.
- [ ] **B**: repeat both, the other way round.
- [ ] Both: write something **shared** and confirm the other sees it.

## 3. Saving and finding

- [ ] Both: capture a thought. Check the date it picked up. Save it.
- [ ] Both: find it again a few minutes later, by browsing and by Search.
- [ ] Both: check the item says what it did with your writing — synced, saved
      on this phone, or needing attention.

## 4. Correction

- [ ] Both: change something you saved. Confirm the change sticks after leaving
      the screen and coming back.
- [ ] Both: correct something the app got wrong about your item — the category,
      the date, who it belongs to. Confirm it stays corrected after a relaunch.

## 5. Shared approval

- [ ] **A**: propose something shared.
- [ ] **B**: it should be clearly labelled as **proposed**, in words, not only
      by colour.
- [ ] **B**: agree to it. Both of you should now see it labelled **agreed**.
- [ ] **A**: change the proposal after B agreed. **B must be asked again.** A
      changed proposal that stays agreed is a serious finding.
- [ ] **A**: withdraw a proposal. It stays withdrawn on both phones, including
      after a relaunch.
- [ ] Both: answering a private question asks permission before anything is
      sent for processing, and changing your answer asks again.

## 6. Offline recovery

Do this properly — it is the part most likely to lose something.

- [ ] Turn on **Airplane Mode**.
- [ ] Write and save two or three things. The app should accept them and say
      they are on this phone.
- [ ] **Force-quit the app** (swipe it away) while still offline.
- [ ] Reopen it. **Everything you wrote is still there.** Anything missing is a
      data-loss finding — report it before doing anything else.
- [ ] Turn Airplane Mode off. Wait.
- [ ] Everything syncs, and **nothing appears twice**. Count them.
- [ ] The other phone sees them.

## 7. Larger text and reaching the keyboard

- [ ] Both: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text, near
      maximum. Open WE.
- [ ] Nothing is cut off, overlapped, or unreadable. Note any screen that is.
- [ ] Open a capture with the keyboard up. **The save buttons must be reachable
      above the keyboard** without dismissing it.
- [ ] Try one-handed on each phone. Note anything you cannot reach.

---

## What to do with what you find

**Immediately, before continuing** — anything in these four:

- One of you could see the other's private material.
- Something you saved is gone.
- Either of you is locked out of your own account.
- Pairing, saving, retrieval or correction does not work at all.

**Everything else** — note it and keep going. Small visual and wording problems
get grouped into a later update; they are not worth interrupting the two weeks
for.

## Reporting

Use **Account → Feedback** in the app. It shows you the whole report before it
sends, and it never includes anything you wrote to each other.

Whether you use it or just message me, five lines:

```
Build:     1.0 (2)
Tried:     what I did
Expected:  what I thought would happen
Actually:  what happened
Blocked:   yes / no
```

Screenshots help and are optional. Never paste anything real either of you
wrote into a report — describe it instead.

## Then: about two weeks

Use it for real. Real thoughts, real plans, no performance for the test.

Note as you go: where you were confused, where WE got something wrong about
you, where it felt like pressure, where it interrupted you for no reason, and
where it actually helped.

At the end, answer these **separately**, without comparing first:

- What did WE take off your mind?
- What did you have to correct?
- Could you find things again?
- Did you always know what was private?
- Did a shared prompt ever feel like pressure?
