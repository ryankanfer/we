# WE Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

WE stores the account information needed to sign people in, relationship content
they choose to save, and limited operational records needed to run the service.
Private answers and notes are owner-only and are never shown to a partner. WE does
not sell personal data or use third-party advertising trackers.

## OpenAI processing

A shared-direction question uses OpenAI only after the person explicitly agrees
for that answer. WE sends the selected answer, the question and available choices,
and up to three lines of shared evidence. WE never sends the optional private note.
OpenAI returns a proposed shared direction, which WE validates before saving. A
person can choose “Not now — don't send” instead.

WE sets `store=false` on every OpenAI Responses API request. OpenAI does not train
its models on API data by default. Unless WE's OpenAI project has approved Zero
Data Retention or Modified Abuse Monitoring, OpenAI may retain API content for up
to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Until WE confirms an enhanced retention setting,
assume that 30-day maximum applies.

## Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database storage, realtime updates, and server
functions. OpenAI processes only the explicitly permitted shared-direction payload
described above. Apple processes information required to distribute the app and
provide system services.

## Retention and deletion

WE retains account and relationship data while the account is active or as needed
to operate the service. Account deletion is available inside the app. Deletion
removes the account and private content; a former partner may retain a sanitized,
read-only archive of content that was already shared. Legal or security obligations
may require limited records to be retained longer.

## Your choices

You can decline any shared-direction question, control the signals WE is allowed
to notice, keep approved wording off external surfaces, sign out, or delete your
account from the Account screen.
